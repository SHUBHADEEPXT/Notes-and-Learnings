# 🎭 DevOps Analogies — Defence Family Stories to Explain Tech

This repository is my **storytelling collection** of DevOps concepts — explained through real analogies from my defence family background.  

Instead of dry definitions, I connect Docker, Terraform, CI/CD, Ansible, Kubernetes, and more to **everyday experiences** like moving houses, family coordination, or packing strategies.

---

## 🌟 Why Analogies?

- **Memory-friendly**: You don’t forget stories as easily as commands
- **Interview-ready**: Explaining Kubernetes Pods with a “house and rooms” analogy sticks
- **Beginner-friendly**: Makes DevOps approachable for non-technical readers
