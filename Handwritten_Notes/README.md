# ✍️ Handwritten Notes – DevOps Learning Archive

This folder contains a deeply personal and ever-growing collection of **handwritten notes** I created during my DevOps journey. These notes are written with pen, pencil, and paper — the old-school way — to retain depth and clarity during revisions.

🧠 Why Handwritten?
Because I believe **writing = understanding**. These notes helped me move from a QA/Support role into DevOps with real clarity — not just remembering definitions, but truly understanding concepts.
